    dasymetric modelling approach that integrates national census data with geospatial
    covariates (e.g., land cover, building footprints, nighttime lights) to disaggregate
    population counts to a 100m grid. Age and gender structures are derived from census
    microdata and applied to the gridded totals to produce sex- and age-disaggregated
    surfaces for each year from 2015 to 2030.
