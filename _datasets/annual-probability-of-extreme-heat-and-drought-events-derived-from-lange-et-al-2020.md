description: Annual probability maps of extreme heat and drought events on a 0.5deg
  grid, derived from Lange et al. (2020) by converting event time series into yearly
  occurrence probabilities for historical baseline and future periods (2030, 2050,
  2080) under RCP2.6 and RCP6.0.
details: This draft dataset was generated by the University of Oxford using the open
  pipeline at https://github.com/nismod/isimip-exposure to transform the Lange et
  al. (2020, Earth's Future, doi:10.1029/2020EF001616) event time series into annual
  probabilities. Drought events are defined as soil moisture below 2.5th percentile
  for >=7 consecutive months, while extreme heat events are defined as concurrent
  exceedance of relative temperature and absolute temperature-humidity thresholds.
  The dataset covers 8 hydrological models forced by 4 GCMs (baseline, RCP2.6, RCP6.0)
  for time periods 2020, 2030, 2050, and 2080 at 0.5deg resolution. Population exposure
  is computed assuming any population within the event footprint is exposed and is
  held constant at 2020 levels using JRC GHSL GHS-POP R2023A. ISIMIP2b inputs and
  outputs are available via ESGF. The dataset is shared under CC0 1.0 and should be
  cited with Lange et al. (2020) and appropriately acknowledge ISIMIP data providers.
