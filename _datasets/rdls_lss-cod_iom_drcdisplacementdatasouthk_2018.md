  description: This dataset presents the results of the fifteenth round of Mobility
    Tracking assessments in the South Kivu province, carried out by the International
    Organization for Migration's (IOM) Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM). Mobility
    Tracking is deployed twice per year in South Kivu, alongside the other eastern
    provinces of the DRC to establish an up to date baseline IDP and IDP Returnee
    population. The data collection for this exercise was conducted between the 5
    August and 4 September 2025, in collaboration with the local NGO World Needs and
    Help and the Division Provinciale des Affaires Humanitaires (DIVAH). During this
    round, a total of 6,193 villages, in 34 health zones, were assessed through consultations
    with 18,564 key informants. 234,557 displaced households (1,201,673 individuals)
    were identified in South Kivu. The majority of the displaced found refuge in the
    territories of Kalehe and Mwenga. 55% of IDPs are female and 45% are male. 40
    per cent are minors aged under 18. 173,790 returnee households (912,692 individuals)
    were identified in South Kivu. Returnees represent the majority of the population
    in the health zones of Minembwe (80%), Minova (73%), and Kalehe (55%), and Bunyakiri
    (29%).
