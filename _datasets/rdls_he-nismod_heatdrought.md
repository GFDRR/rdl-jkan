description: "Annual probability maps of extreme heat and drought events on a 0.5\xB0\
  \ grid, derived from Lange et al. (2020) by converting event time series into yearly\
  \ occurrence probabilities for historical baseline and future periods (2030, 2050,\
  \ 2080) under RCP2.6 and RCP6.0."
details: "This draft dataset was generated by the University of Oxford using the open\
  \ pipeline at https://github.com/nismod/isimip-exposure to transform the Lange et\
  \ al. (2020, Earth's Future, doi:10.1029/2020EF001616) event time series into annual\
  \ probabilities. Drought events are defined as soil-moisture below 2.5th percentile\
  \ for >=7 consecutive months, while extreme heat events are defined as concurrent\
  \ exceedance of relative temperature and absolute temperature-humidity thresholds.\
  \ The dataset covers 8 hydrological models forced by 4 GCMs (baseline, RCP2.6, RCP6.0)\
  \ for time periods 2020, 2030, 2050, and 2080 at 0.5\xB0 resolution. Population\
  \ exposure is computed assuming any population within the event footprint is exposed\
  \ and is held constant at 2020 levels using JRC GHSL GHS-POP R2023A. ISIMIP2b inputs\
  \ and outputs are available via ESGF. The dataset is shared under CC0 1.0 and should\
  \ be cited with Lange et al. (2020) and appropriately acknowledge ISIMIP data providers."

  description: Annual probability grids for extreme heat and drought events derived
    from bias-corrected ISIMIP2b climate impact model outputs. Daily maximum temperature
    from multiple CMIP5 GCMs was bias-corrected against ERA5 reanalysis to produce
    extreme heat probability surfaces. Drought probability was computed from ISIMIP2b
    land surface model soil moisture and runoff outputs. Population exposure was calculated
    by intersecting event grids with GHS-POP R2023A gridded population using the ISIMIP
    Exposure Processing Pipeline.
