description: Protected areas Natura 2000
details: Natura 2000 is the European Union's key instrument for biodiversity protection,
  comprising sites designated under the Birds Directive (Special Protection Areas
  or SPAs) and the Habitats Directive (Sites of Community Importance or SCIs and Special
  Areas of Conservation or SACs). The European database is updated annually based
  on information submitted by Member States through site-specific standard data forms
  (SDFs), which provide details on designated species, habitat types, and site boundaries.
  The European Environment Agency (EEA) validates all submitted data and identifies
  inconsistencies, though final decisions on corrections remain with Member States
  before database updates. The dataset contains both spatial boundaries and descriptive
  information for each site, including the officially designated species and habitat
  types. The validation process is reflected in updates to the biogeographical Union
  Lists of Sites of Community Importance. Additional web services provide detailed
  information on species and habitat types within Natura 2000 sites, supporting conservation
  planning, environmental impact assessment, and EU environmental policy implementation.
