description: 'Based on requests from ECHO partners, REACH has worked to compile and
  map the location of cyclone shelters in Ukhia and Teknaf in the two Upazilas most
  affected by the Rohingya refugee influx of August 2017, in order to orient and inform
  humanitarian actors working in these locations. This database includes three lists
  of shelters: First, a list of shelters as designated on lists provided by the Cox''s
  Bazar District Relief and Rehabilitation Officer (DRRO). Second, a list of cyclone
  shelters designated by the DRRO with additional structures reported to be shelters
  according to lists provided by the Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP), the District
  Disaster Managemen Plan (DDMP), Upazila Nirbahi Officers (UNOs), UNDP, IOM, UNHCR,
  WFP, the Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), and the Comprehensive Disaster
  Management Programme (CDMP). The third list mirrors the second list but also includes
  all of alternative names used by different agencies.. [Source: This metadata record
  was automatically extracted from the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX); Original
  dataset: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cox-s-bazar-district-teknaf-and-ukhiya-upazila-cyclone-shelter-locations]'
details: 'All lists: None of the different databases used as the basis for this exercise
  employs a consistent system of unique identifier codes that would allow for effective
  matching of records between databases. As a result, in many cases different databases
  have used different names and terminologies to refer to the same shelters. Wherever
  possible REACH has worked to match the same shelters across different lists using
  GPS triangulation, fuzzy matching approaches, and site visits. However, given the
  limitations of the data matches are not warranted to be 100% accurate in all cases
  and further verification may be required.'
