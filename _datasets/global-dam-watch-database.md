details: The Global Dam Watch database is a comprehensive, open source database of
  river barriers and reservoirs worldwide. It centralizes and harmonizes multiple
  datasets to support large-scale hydrological and environmental analyses. The database
  contains over 41,000 barrier locations and more than 35,000 associated reservoirs,
  providing information on storage capacity, surface area, and other key attributes.
  Each barrier and reservoir is geolocated and linked to global hydrographic networks,
  such as HydroSHEDS and HydroLAKES. The GDW database is regularly updated and freely
  available to support research, policy, and conservation efforts.
