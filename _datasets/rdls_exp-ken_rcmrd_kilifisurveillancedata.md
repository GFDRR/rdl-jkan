description: 'This dataset provides records of pest/disease occurrences in maize/bean/potato
  across Kilifi County in Kenya collected under the Risk Management System for Plant
  Pests and Diseases project. The data includes details on the geographich location,
  date of occurrence, type of crop affected, type of pest/disease, stage of growth/severity
  of disease. It integrates surveillance reports, Earth observation data, and field
  survey information to support early warning, response planning, and risk mitigation
  strategies. The dataset is designed to aid policymakers, researchers, and farmers
  in monitoring trends and making informed decisions to manage the pest/disease risk
  and therefore enhance food security. It is available in geospatial formats, ensuring
  compatibility with mapping and decision-support tools. Data accuracy was ensured
