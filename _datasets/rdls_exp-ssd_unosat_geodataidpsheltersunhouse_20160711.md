  UN House compound in Juba, Central Equatoria, South Sudan. UNITAR-UNOSAT analysis
  of WorldView-1 satellite imagery acquired 27 June 2016 revealed a total of 8,477
  shelters as well as 231 infrastructure and support buildings within the compound.
  This represents an increase of approximately 3.2 percent in shelters and a decrease
  of roughly 3.3 percent in infrastructure and support buildings since the previous
  UNITAR-UNOSAT analysis of 25 September 2015 satellite imagery. While no structures
  were detected within PoC2, containers were visible in this area on 27 June 2016,
