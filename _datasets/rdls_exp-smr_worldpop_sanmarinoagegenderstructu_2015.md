  description: "Population estimates were generated by WorldPop using a Random Forest-based\
    \ dasymetric disaggregation model that redistributes national census and administrative\
    \ population counts into gridded surfaces at approximately 100m resolution, incorporating\
    \ geospatial covariates such as land cover, building footprints, and road networks.\
    \ Age and gender structures are applied to the total population grid using demographic\
    \ data from national statistics and UN population projections for the period 2015\u2013\
    2030. The resulting GeoTIFF rasters provide annual, sex- and age-disaggregated\
    \ population counts per grid cell for use in exposure assessment and disaster\
    \ risk analysis."
